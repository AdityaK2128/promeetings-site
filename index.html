Meeting notes that write themselves.

Record a meeting, or import one you already have. Pro Meetings listens to the audio itself — tone, pace, hesitation, interruptions — and gives you a speaker-separated transcript, the decisions that were made, the actions people committed to, and formal minutes you can send.

What it does

  • Transcript with speakers separated by voice, and crosstalk flagged rather than guessed at
  • Summary, action items with owners and deadlines, and a decision register
  • Sentiment across the meeting, who dominated and who barely spoke
  • Editable minutes, exportable as PDF or Word
  • Ask questions about the meeting afterwards

Your recordings

Recordings are uploaded only so they can be analysed, and are never sold, never used for advertising, and never fed to tracking of any kind. You can erase everything from inside the app. The privacy policy sets out the details.
