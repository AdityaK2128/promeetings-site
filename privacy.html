Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

Pro Meetings records meetings and uses AI to produce transcripts, summaries and insights. Doing that means handling recordings of conversations, which is about as sensitive as app data gets. This page explains exactly what is collected, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

In short

Your recordings are uploaded to our cloud only so they can be analysed, and are processed by Google's Gemini AI. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and there is no tracking or analytics in the app. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Calendar

If you grant calendar access, the app reads the titles and times of today's events purely to suggest a name for a recording. This happens entirely on your device. Calendar data is never uploaded, stored or shared, and the permission is optional — declining it only means you type the title yourself.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos or files other than an audio file you explicitly choose to import.
  • We never see your payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

How your recordings are processed

When you ask for a meeting to be analysed, the audio is uploaded to Firebase Storage under a folder that only your account can read, and is then sent to Google's Gemini API. Gemini returns the transcript and analysis, which are saved to your device and, if you are signed in, to your account.

Analysis runs on our server rather than in the app, so the AI credentials are never present on your device. Requests are attested to confirm they come from a genuine copy of the app.

Google processes this data as our service provider in order to return a result. Their handling is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Gemini API terms.

Recording other people

Recording a conversation may require the consent of everyone taking part, and the law differs by country and by state. You are responsible for obtaining that consent before you record. Please tell the people in the room.

How long we keep things

  • Audio, free accounts: removed from cloud storage 30 days after upload. The transcript, summary, insights and minutes are kept, and any copy already on your device stays on your device.
  • Audio, Pro subscribers: kept while your subscription is active, since cloud backup is part of what Pro provides.
  • Transcripts and analysis: kept until you delete the meeting or your account.
  • Account details: kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

Deleting a meeting in the app removes it from your device and, if it was synced, from the cloud along with its audio.

To remove everything, open Account and choose Delete Account. This permanently erases your recordings, your transcripts and analysis, and your account itself. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit, and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Access rules are enforced server-side so that one account cannot read another account's recordings or analysis.
  • Your usage allowance and subscription status are held server-side and cannot be modified from a device.

No system is perfectly secure, but we do not ask you to take our word for it: the checks above are enforced by the server rather than by the app.

Children

Pro Meetings is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. The in-app account deletion covers erasure immediately. For anything else, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL and we will respond within 30 days.

Data is processed on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
