Support

Something not working, or a question the answers below don't cover? Email ak2128@proton.me and we'll get back to you.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and roughly when it happened. If it involves a specific meeting, its length and whether it was recorded or imported is useful too.

Recording

Can I lock my phone or use another app while recording?

Yes. Recording continues in the background, and a live timer appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island so you can see it is still running.

What stops a recording?

Force-quitting the app — swiping it away in the app switcher — shuts it down and ends the recording. No app can prevent that. For very long sessions, record in Apple's Voice Memos app instead and import the file afterwards; you get exactly the same analysis.

A phone call came in. Did I lose the recording?

No. Recording pauses for the call and everything captured beforehand is kept.

Is there a length limit?

A single recording stops and saves automatically at three hours. For longer sessions, record in Voice Memos and import the file.

Analysis

Why is analysis taking so long?

Long recordings genuinely take a few minutes. The screen shows which stage it has reached — uploading, with a percentage, then analysing — along with a timer. You can leave the screen and come back; it keeps going.

Analysis failed. What now?

Open the meeting and tap Retry. The audio is already uploaded, so it will go straight to analysis. If it fails repeatedly, check your connection and email us.

Which audio files can I import?

M4A, MP3, WAV, AIFF and MP4, up to 300 MB.

The speakers are labelled wrong.

Open the Transcript tab and tap Name Speakers to give each one a real name. It updates the transcript, the insights and the minutes everywhere they appear.

Minutes and exports

Can I correct the minutes before sending them?

Yes. Open the Minutes tab and tap Edit. You can fix names, reword a decision, add someone who did not speak, and write notes. Your edits are kept and are never overwritten by re-analysis.

What formats can I export?

PDF and Word (.docx) for both the full report and the minutes, plus plain text for pasting into an email. Documents exported by Pro subscribers carry no Pro Meetings branding.

Account and subscription

How many free analyses do I get?

Five. The first five include every Pro feature so you can try everything. After that, free accounts get five analyses a month.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

I subscribed but Pro isn't showing.

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account and choose Delete Account. This permanently erases your recordings, transcripts and analysis. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription — do that separately in Settings.

What happens to my audio over time?

On the free plan the original audio is removed from cloud backup after 30 days. Your transcripts, summaries, insights and minutes are kept, and anything on your device stays there. Pro keeps the audio backed up as well.
